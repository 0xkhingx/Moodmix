Moodie

The MoodMix mascot. Character sheet v2: arms and guide poses. Flat style matched to the app's MoodFaces.

Role

A mood buddy: it listens (headphones, eyes closed), feels your mood (turns that mood's colour) and vibes when the music lands.

Rules

  • Resting colour: Cuzzon lilac ; mood colour only when it feels something.
  • Ink #1A1712, no outlines on the body; shine like the MoodFaces.
  • Motion: squash and stretch on landings, head-bob on beats, blinks. No idle wobble.
  • Signature sound: one soft "boop" when it reacts (a real recording, chosen later).
